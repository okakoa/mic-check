About MicCheck

MicCheck is a free online microphone test for anyone on a phone, tablet, or computer: press one button to see and hear whether your microphone is working.

Why it exists

Right before a video call, a class, or a recording, you need one quick answer: does my microphone work? Many sites bury that answer under menus, sign-ups, and settings. MicCheck does one job, gives one plain answer, and lets you hear a short playback.

How it protects your privacy

The test runs entirely inside your browser using standard web technology. Your audio is never uploaded or stored on a server. The microphone is released as soon as the test ends. Read the full privacy policy.

Who runs it

MicCheck is an independent project built and maintained by One Page Web Apps, focused on fast, single-purpose browser tools that work without accounts, downloads, or clutter. The site is funded by advertising so it can stay free.
