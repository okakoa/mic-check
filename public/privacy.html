Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The short version

The microphone test runs entirely in your browser. Your audio is never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with anyone. We use cookies only for analytics and advertising, and you can refuse them.

Your microphone and audio

  • Audio is processed on your device with your browser's built-in Web Audio API.
  • The short playback clip is held only in your browser's memory and is discarded when you reload or leave the page.
  • We do not receive, record, or keep any audio. The microphone is released as soon as the test ends.
  • Microphone names shown on the page are read by your browser and never sent to us.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This site may use:

  • A consent choice stored in your browser's local storage, so we remember whether you accepted or rejected cookies.
  • Analytics cookies from Google Analytics, to count visits and see which parts of the page are used.
  • Advertising cookies from Google AdSense or other ad partners, to show and measure ads.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent first. Until you accept, analytics and advertising storage are set to "denied" using Google Consent Mode.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people use the site and whether the test succeeds (for example, "working" or "blocked"). No audio and no device names are sent. Google may process data such as your approximate location, device type, and browser. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense or other third-party ad networks such as Adsterra or Monetag. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Read how Google uses cookies in advertising.

How to change your choice

Clear this site's data in your browser settings (cookies and site data) and reload the page to see the consent choice again. You can also block cookies entirely in your browser; the microphone test still works without them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we do not collect audio or accounts, most data is held by Google and our ad partners; contact us and we will help.
