# MicCheck – 8-Second Short-Form Video Campaign & Production Script

This document details the production flow, script variants, weekly launch run, and social response playbook for short vertical videos (9:16) targeting **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, and **YouTube Shorts**.

---

## 1. The Core 8-Second Screen Recording (The "Master Clip")

You only need to record **one clean 8-second master vertical screen recording (1080×1920)** on a phone (or browser mobile view). Every video variation uses this same clip with different visual hooks, text overlays, and audio cues.

### Master Screen Recording Timeline (8 Seconds Total)

| Timestamp | Visual Action on Screen | Audio / Sound Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0s – 1.5s** | Finger taps the blue **"Test my microphone"** button. | Click / UI tap sound. |
| **1.5s – 4.5s** | Green meter pulses rapidly (`Input level: 78 / 100`) while voice/clap sound is detected. "Say something or clap now..." | Short spoken voice ("Can you hear me now?") or distinct snap/clap. |
| **4.5s – 6.5s** | Big bold green result pops up: **"✅ Yes: your microphone is working."** | Subtle high-tech chime / success ping. |
| **6.5s – 8.0s** | Finger taps **"Play it back"** → immediate instant voice replay confirms sound. | Clean voice audio replay from the phone speaker. |

## 4. The "Where People Complain" Organic Playbook (No Pitching)

Instead of posting promo ads, monitor community threads where people are actively asking why their mic isn't working. **Answer with the tool as an instant diagnostic step.**

### High-Volume Target Communities
- **Reddit:**
  - `r/techsupport` (Daily posts: "Headset mic not detected", "Mic quiet in Discord")
  - `r/discordapp` (Frequent posts: "Nobody can hear me but mic works in Windows")
  - `r/zoom` / `r/MicrosoftTeams` (Posts: "Can't hear me on Zoom call")
  - `r/pcmasterrace` / `r/headphones`
- **Twitter/X & Threads:**
  - Search queries: `"mic not working"`, `"why can nobody hear me"`, `"zoom mic broken"`
- **Discord Help Channels & Gaming Forums**

### Reply Scripts (Helpful, Problem-Solving, Zero Sales Hype)

If you hear yourself there, your hardware is fine and it's just Discord/Zoom's input permissions or device selection holding the feed."
